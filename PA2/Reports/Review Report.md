In addition to reviewing progress from the last sprint, our team also discussed inefficient practices regarding Jira and the Scrum framework in general.

The main issue stemmed from the mid-sprint redistribution of effort. Task reassignments and team communication were not handled properly, resulting in Jira work items that did not accurately reflect the actual progress. Additionally, some team members did not update their task status in a timely manner, either moving tasks directly from “To Do” to “Review” or skipping “Review” entirely.

These issues would lead to the fact that there is some discrepancy between the Jira task log and what is reported in the Weekly Report. Our group would be more than happy to answer any question from the instructor.
